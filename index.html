About Me

Hi, I'm Sadman Sakib Alif, currently pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Bologna, Italy, as a recipient of the MAECI Scholarship awarded by the Italian Government.

Previously, I served as an Adjunct Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, Bangladesh, and as a Lab Instructor at North South University.

My research focuses on Machine Learning for Healthcare, particularly medical image analysis, multimodal learning, and explainable artificial intelligence. I am also interested in Physical AI and Robotics, with an emphasis on integrating intelligent models into real-world systems.
